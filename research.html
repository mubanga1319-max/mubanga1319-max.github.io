Research in progress

Help document our clans

I am recording clan traditions across the Bemba-speaking peoples. Elders, families and young people can all contribute.

The research covers the Bemba of Chitimukulu, the Ushi, the Chishinga, Kabende, Ng'umbo, Mukulu and Unga, the Lunda of Mwata Kazembe, the Bisa, the Lala, the Tabwa and the Lungu. Each people has its own history, and all share one clan system.

Takes 3 minutes

Tell us your clan

Write your clan's name in your own language, say what it means, and tell us your language and village. Every answer helps build a complete list of our clans.

Fill in the form

What I am asking

  1. Which clan is the oldest, and how do you know? Which comes second, and third?
  2. What does your clan's totem mean, and is there a story about how it got its name?
  3. Which clans are your joking partners (abanungwe), and what do they do at funerals and weddings?
  4. Which village and chief did your family belong to?
  5. Have you heard a different account from someone else? What was it?

How contributions are handled

  • Nothing is recorded or published without the contributor's consent.
  • Contributors choose whether their name is used.
  • Different accounts are all recorded; disagreement between elders is part of the history.
  • Private family matters stay private.
