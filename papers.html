Policy brief · October 2026

Recognising the Clan: A Proposal for an Optional Clan Field on Zambia's National Registration Card

As Zambia moves to the digital NRC, an optional, self-declared clan field would give civic recognition to an identity that links citizens across provinces, languages and parties. The brief sets out the proposal, its safeguards under the Data Protection Act, and a roadmap for the Ministry of Home Affairs, the House of Chiefs and Parliament.

Essay · August 2026

Clans, Not Colonial Borders: Why Zambia Must Officially Recognise Totems to Heal Its Political Divisions

Provinces were drawn for colonial administration, and our politics still follows their lines. Clans never did. This essay argues that the one identity colonial rule never reorganised could help Zambia move beyond inherited divisions.
