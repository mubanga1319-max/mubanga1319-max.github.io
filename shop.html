Ndi mwina nani? Wear your answer.

A T-shirt printed with your own clan map: your clan at the centre, and the clans of your four grandparents around you, each with its totem.

Choose your colour

Every design comes in black or white. The front carries your clan map; the back asks Ndi mwina nani? Which clan am I?

How it works

  1. Find your four clans. Ask your elders the clans of your four grandparents. Your own clan is your mother's mother's clan.
  2. Send your order on WhatsApp with your size, colour (black or white), the four clans and your town.
  3. We design your shirt and send you a picture to approve before printing.
  4. We print and deliver.
