Ilyashi pa Nsaka · Indigenous knowledge of the Bemba-speaking peoples

Before we became provinces, we already belonged to something larger.

Essays and research on the clans, words and ceremonies of the Bemba-speaking peoples of Zambia, and a guide for young people who want to trace their roots.

Ndi mwina nani? Which clan am I?

The nsaka is where knowledge was passed on through conversation. This is a modern nsaka, with a seat for everyone: elders and young people, women and men, and every people and clan.
